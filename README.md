# Personal Brand Landing Page

This website serves as a **landing page** for your personal brand, focusing on driving traffic, building an audience, and promoting your documentaries. The primary goal is to collect viewer emails and send them detailed blogs (newsletters) about your documentaries.

---

## **Design Specifications**

### **Fonts**
The website uses a clean and modern font system to reflect professionalism and creativity.

#### **Primary Font (Headings):** `Poppins`
- **Usage:** Used for all headings (H1, H2, H3, etc.).
- **Style:** 
  - **Bold** for main headings (e.g., hero section, section titles).
  - **Medium** for subheadings.
- **Purpose:** Provides a modern, clean, and professional look.

#### **Secondary Font (Body Text):** `Roboto`
- **Usage:** Used for all body text, paragraphs, and descriptions.
- **Style:** 
  - **Regular** for standard text.
  - **Medium** for emphasized text or captions.
- **Purpose:** Ensures readability and complements the primary font.

---

### **Color Palette**
The website uses a cohesive color palette to create a visually appealing and professional design.

#### **Base Colors**
- **Light Gray:** `#F4F4F4`
  - **Usage:** Background color for sections and overall website.
- **Dark Gray:** `#2C2C2C`
  - **Usage:** Text color and accents for contrast.

#### **Primary Color**
- **Electric Blue:** `#00B4D8`
  - **Usage:** Primary accent color for headings, buttons, and links.

#### **Secondary Colors**
- **Coral:** `#FF6F61`
  - **Usage:** Secondary accent color for call-to-action buttons and highlights.
- **Soft Green:** `#A8D8B9`
  - **Usage:** Accent color for additional highlights or backgrounds.

#### **Text Colors**
- **Dark Gray:** `#2C2C2C`
  - **Usage:** Primary text color for light backgrounds.
- **Light Gray:** `#F4F4F4`
  - **Usage:** Primary text color for dark backgrounds.

---

## **Website Features**

### **1. Hero Section**
- **Tagline:** A compelling headline that introduces your brand and documentaries (e.g., “Exploring the World Through My Lens”).
- **Subheadline:** A short description of your work and what viewers can expect from your newsletters.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):**
  - A prominent email signup form with a button (e.g., “Subscribe for Exclusive Content”).
  - Example placeholder: “Enter your email to join the journey.”

### **2. About Me**
- **Introduction:**
  - A short bio about you, your passion for documentaries, and your unique perspective.
- **Photo:**
  - A professional or casual photo of you to build a personal connection.
- **Why I Create:**
  - A paragraph explaining your motivation for creating documentaries and sharing stories.

### **3. Documentaries Showcase**
- **Featured Documentaries:**
  - A grid or carousel showcasing your most impactful documentaries.
  - Each item includes:
    - Thumbnail image.
    - Title and short description.
    - Link to watch the documentary (e.g., YouTube or Vimeo).
- **CTA:** “Subscribe to get behind-the-scenes insights and updates.”

### **4. Newsletter Signup Section**
- **Headline:** “Get Exclusive Content Delivered to Your Inbox.”
- **Description:** Explain the value of subscribing (e.g., behind-the-scenes stories, early access, or exclusive insights).
- **Email Form:**
  - Simple form with an email field and a submit button (e.g., “Subscribe Now”).
- **Privacy Assurance:** A short note assuring users their email will not be shared.

### **5. Blog/Newsletter Teaser**
- **Headline:** “What’s in the Newsletter?”
- **Description:** Highlight the type of content subscribers will receive (e.g., detailed blogs, behind-the-scenes stories, upcoming projects).
- **CTA:** “Subscribe to Stay Updated.”

### **6. Contact Section**
- **Headline:** “Let’s Connect.”
- **Description:** Invite visitors to reach out for collaborations, questions, or feedback.
- **Contact Form:**
  - Fields for name, email, subject, and message.
- **Social Media Links:**
  - Icons linking to your YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, etc.

## **Usage of Fonts and Colors**

### **Font Usage**
- **Headings:** Use `Poppins Bold` for main headings and `Poppins Medium` for subheadings.
- **Body Text:** Use `Roboto Regular` for paragraphs and `Roboto Medium` for emphasized text.

### **Color Usage**
- **Backgrounds:**
  - Use `Light Gray (#F4F4F4)` for most sections.
  - Use `Dark Gray (#2C2C2C)` for contrast sections (e.g., footer or testimonials).
- **Headings and Links:**
  - Use `Electric Blue (#00B4D8)` for primary accents.
- **Buttons and Highlights:**
  - Use `Coral (#FF6F61)` for CTAs (e.g., subscribe buttons).
  - Use `Soft Green (#A8D8B9)` for secondary accents.
- **Text:**
  - Light Backgrounds: Use `Dark Gray (#2C2C2C)`.
  - Dark Backgrounds: Use `Light Gray (#F4F4F4)`.

---

## **Development Notes**
- **Responsive Design:** Ensure the website is mobile-friendly and adapts to different screen sizes.
- **SEO Optimization:** Use proper meta tags, alt text for images, and structured data for better search engine visibility.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure sufficient contrast between text and background colors for readability.
- **Email Integration:** Use a service like Mailchimp or ConvertKit to handle email collection and newsletter distribution.

## **Example Layout**
1. **Header:** Logo, navigation menu (Home, About, Documentaries, Contact).
2. **Hero Section:** Tagline, subheadline, email signup form.
3. **About Me:** Bio, photo, and personal story.
4. **Documentaries Showcase:** Featured documentaries with thumbnails and descriptions.
5. **Newsletter Signup:** Headline, description, email form.
6. **Blog Teaser:** Highlight newsletter content and CTA.
